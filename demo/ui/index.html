Why this matters

Tasdiq sits between the bank and the instant-payment rail. Before money moves, it asks the telecom network three questions (Has the SIM changed? Is this number really on this device? Is the device roaming?) plus the bank's own behavior data (Is this payee new? Is the amount unusual?). A deterministic engine decides in a fraction of a second — the AI never decides; it writes the explanation and the regulator report afterwards. Result: fraud caught before the money moves, honest customers don't get OTP friction.
